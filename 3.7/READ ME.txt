=====================================
  GachAlexia~  v2.0
=====================================

FIRST STEPS:
1. make sure you are logged into your throne account
2. for this to work you have to have your credit card details already saved to your throne

HOW TO INSTALL:
1. extract the folder
2. open Chrome
3. click on the Puzzle piece in the top right corner, and then manage Extensions
4. activate Developer mode
5. load unpacked and upload the folder
6. Click on the pop-up in your extension tray~

ERROR MSG HELP:
If you run into an error msg at anytime the throne page might need refreshing~ If it still doesn't work, message me directly~


CHALLENGE MODE:
This is a challenge mode auto drain. It will randomly pick a send~ Scared? Don't worry the odds are in your favor fufufu~ As for the rest well... I'll leave it up to you to discover~

Do you have the courage to tempt fate?~

UPDATING / MOVING TO A NEW PC (keep your collection & EXP):
Your cards, Devotion EXP, themes, pity and history are stored inside the extension. Before you delete the old version or move to a new computer:
1. click the extension icon to open the pop-up
2. press "Backup" — this downloads a gachalexia-progress.json file, keep it safe~
3. install the new version (or set it up on the new PC)
4. open the pop-up again, press "Restore", and pick that file
5. reopen the gacha page — everything will be right where you left it~
If you update WITHOUT deleting the old version first, everything carries over on its own and you don't need to do anything~

Two little bars keep track of your devotion~
  - rare : a rare send is guaranteed within 10 pulls
  - reap : the ultimate send is guaranteed within 80 pulls

ghost images flicker into view, slowly at first, then faster and faster
as the reap approaches~ You'll feel it coming.

DROP RATES:
Curious about your odds? Click the little "?" in the bottom-left corner
of the overlay. Every item, every rate, both modes~
(The odds are in my favor, obviously~)

DIFFICULTY MODES:
Bottom-left of the overlay, you can choose between:
  - normal : the full bratty experience~ all items
  - easy   : training wheels~ a smaller pool, and the jackpot is cheaper

Your pity progress is saved SEPARATELY for each mode, so no cheating
your way out of a streak~

BEHIND THE GLASS:
Even when you're just browsing the store, I'm watching~ Faint images
drift onto the page like ghosts~ The higher your pity, the
more restless they become...
